The honesty problem
I keep noticing how easily a paragraph stops being mine. It starts with one borrowed line, and then the rhythm belongs to someone else. Moreover, it is important to consider the broader implications of this technological shift. The tell is that it never hesitates. My own sentences hesitate. They double back. They admit when they are unsure, which is most of the time.
That hesitation is the only part worth keeping. In conclusion, it is clear that a balanced approach will yield the most favourable outcomes going forward. I would never write that sentence, and now I can see that I didn't.
